What Sets Sale Order Payment Progress Apart?

The Sale Order Payment Progress module gives your sales and management teams complete real-time visibility into order payment status. Instead of navigating to accounting, users can check payment states (Not Paid, Partial, Fully Paid, Overdue) and register payments directly from the Sales Order form. It features dynamic visual ribbons, tree-view badges, custom search filters, and automatic due date tracking, streamlining the order-to-cash process.

Explore The Key Features

Discover how our module simplifies tracking and registering payments directly from Sales Orders.

Odoo 19 Compliant Payment States

Tracks Not Paid, Partial Paid, Fully Paid, and Overdue statuses in real-time, matching standard Odoo 19 accounting states perfectly.

Immediate Register Payment Action

A dedicated highlight action button appears directly on the Sales Order form when related invoices are unpaid, allowing quick payments.

Accurate Outstanding Amount Due

Computes residual and confirmed payment amounts dynamically, taking into account tax inclusions, currencies, and partial reconciliation logs.

Interactive Payment Reconciled Info

Embeds a payment details popover directly on the Sales Order screen to let users inspect reconciled journals, dates, and payment methods.

Color-Coded Status Ribbons & Badges

Displays clear visual banners on form views (e.g. Red ribbon for "No Invoice") and badges on tree/list views for instant order status recognition.

Automatic Overdue Alerts & Tracking

Monitors invoice due dates and highlights overdue orders with elapsed day counters to alert sales teams and credit controllers.

Custom Search View & Filters

Equips the standard Sales Order search panel with filters and grouping rules specifically designed for monitoring payment status.

Pre-Delivery & Post-Delivery Modes

Intelligently handles order modifications and lines status variations, ensuring all active invoicing conditions are tracked.

See It In Action

Step 1: Create a Sales Order. Initially, the payment status ribbon displays as "No Invoice" and the order amount due equals the total order value.

Step 2: Create and validate a customer invoice. The status ribbon automatically updates to "Not Paid".

Step 3: Register a partial payment. The status ribbon transitions to "Partial Paid" and the residual amount due updates instantly.

Step 4: Complete the remaining payment. The ribbon transitions to "Fully Paid" and the payment breakdown widget logs the reconciled history.

Find answers to common questions about the Sale Order Payment Progress module.

4. How does the Sale Order Payment Progress tracking work?

The module tracks payment progress by looking at all invoices associated with the Sales Order. It computes payment status as "No Invoice", "Not Paid", "Partial Paid", "Fully Paid", or "Overdue".

5. How can I register a payment directly from the Sales Order?

If there are unpaid invoices, a "Register Payment" button appears directly on the Sales Order form. Clicking this button lets you record a payment immediately without navigating to the Accounting app.

6. What determines if a Sales Order is marked as "Overdue"?

If any of the confirmed invoices linked to the Sales Order has passed its due date and remains unpaid or partially paid, the order's payment status is updated to "Overdue" with the days elapsed.

7. What is the "No Invoice" red ribbon on the Sales Order form?

When a Sales Order is confirmed but no customer invoices have been generated yet, a red "No Invoice" ribbon is displayed on the form view to alert the sales team.

8. Can I view payment breakdown information on the Sales Order?

Yes, the module embeds a reconciled payments popover widget and displays the residual amount (amount due) and paid amount directly in the Sales Order's order total section.

9. Which Odoo versions and editions are supported?

This module is designed for Odoo 19 (Community and Enterprise editions) and requires the standard Sales and Invoicing modules.

10. Does this module support multi-currency sales orders?

Yes. It accurately computes paid and residual balances according to the currency defined on the Sales Order, handling exchange rates and currency conversions seamlessly.
